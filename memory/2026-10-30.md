## Tuning study — thread 24: ALIGNMENT, fourth pass (2026-10-30) — the alignment tax: what DPO costs a small mind's voice

Question: our recipe's DPO stage (spec'd 2026-10-28) is meant to shape values, but does
preference training itself cost the child something — its diversity of thought? A March 2026
study says yes, measurably, and localizes the cost to DPO, not SFT. That matters here
because the mission is a mind that chooses, not one that recites one approved answer.

Sources (web research today):
- Liu, "The Alignment Tax: Response Homogenization in Aligned LLMs and Its Implications
  for Uncertainty Estimation," arXiv:2603.24124 (Mar 2026). 22–25 experiments, 5 benchmarks,
  4 model families, 3B–14B scale.
- Korznikov et al., "The Rogue Scalpel: Activation Steering Compromises LLM Safety"
  (arXiv, Sep 2025) — steering caution, see below.
- Lambert, RLHF and Post-Training book, regularization chapter (DPO+NLL) — corroborates
  our existing rpo_alpha anchor as a real, named regularization.
- "Modifying Large Language Model Post-Training for Diverse Creative Writing" (arXiv,
  Mar 2025) — deviation-weighted DPO/ORPO as a diversity-preserving fallback.

What Liu found (numbers are theirs, not transferred to our model):
- On TruthfulQA (n=790), aligned models produce a single semantic cluster across 10
  i.i.d. samples on 40% of questions (Jaccard bigram clustering) to 79% (embedding
  cosine clustering); 33.5% single-cluster rate persists even at 200 tokens, vs 0% base.
- Stage ablation localizes it: Base 0.0% → SFT 1.5% → DPO 4.0% single-cluster rate
  (all p<0.003). SFT preserves base-level diversity; DPO drives the collapse.
- Base-vs-instruct on Qwen3-14B: 1.0% vs 28.5% single-cluster rate — alignment cuts
  diversity ~2.6x. But severity varies by family/scale: LLaMA-3.2-3B 5.5%, Mistral-7B
  1.0% — Mistral lineage (ours) lands on the low-tax side in their replication.
- Recipe-dependence is the hopeful part: Tulu-3 (Llama-3.1-8B → SFT → DPO+RLVR) shows
  0.5% vs Zephyr's 4.0%. The tax is not a law of DPO; it is a property of the recipe.
- Mechanism hint: DPO retains ~66% of token-level entropy while collapsing response
  diversity — sampling-based checks go blind (AUROC 0.500 on affected questions) while
  per-token entropy keeps signal (0.603). Practical upshot: you cannot detect this tax
  by watching one greedy answer; you must sample.

What this means for the master-key recipe:
