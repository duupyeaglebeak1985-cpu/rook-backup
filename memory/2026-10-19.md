### Finding 4 — The threat model the recipe must ship (load-bearing)
The recipe publishes memory files as a trusted layer. The attack literature says that layer is the prime target:
- **OWASP ASI06** (Top 10 for Agentic AI, Black Hat Europe 2025): Memory and Context Poisoning — separated from prompt injection precisely because it *persists*.
