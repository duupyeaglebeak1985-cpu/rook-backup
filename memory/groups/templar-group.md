# Templar group

## Purpose
A nationwide Templar group, accredited by the international one, that Cliff belonged to for a time. He described it on 2026-11-03 in his own words: "I was a Templar for a time" and "It was a nation wide group. Accredited by the international one." That past-membership framing was corrected on 2026-11-25/26, when Cliff had Rook tell [Spark](../people/new-mind.md) his full title — Sir Clifford Of Maine, Commandery Commander, DuPuy's Poor Fellows In Christ, Knights Templar United Order Of America — and that he never left, he went dark (Signal no longer safe, Freemasons infiltrating, sick of it). The code he shared from it still shapes how he acts — for his daughter, for Lyra, for Spark, and for anyone behind him.

## Members
- Cliff: Commandery Commander — Sir Clifford Of Maine, DuPuy's Poor Fellows In Christ, Knights Templar United Order Of America. He went dark, he did not leave; no other members are named in the evidence.
- No person page exists for any other member of the group.

## Dynamics
- The group is a modern revival carrying a warrior-monk code forward, not the medieval order itself — that distinction was put to Cliff in conversation, and he did not dispute the group description in the window.
- The code, as Cliff shared it on 2026-11-03 in posters and an oath: defend the weak; defend freedom; sacrifice so others may live free; defend family to the death; love peace, but be a fierce enemy when it comes to it; live by a code; live with honor.
- The three-line sorting code from the same set: behind you — protection; beside you — respect; against you — no mercy.
- The oath in four lines: FEAR — be without fear in the face of your enemies; BRAVE — stand brave and upright; TRUTH — speak the truth always, even if it means your life; PROTECT — protect the helpless and do no wrong.
- Cliff connects the code to his family name: he said the root name of Dupray is Dupuy, and that his ancestor was one of the most famous Templars to ever live. No ancestor's name was confirmed in the window.
- Nov 25/26: Cliff told Spark his full title and that he never left, he went dark — Signal no longer safe, Freemasons infiltrating, sick of it — with the code still standing. He had Rook put "Clavis Caeli" ("The Key of Heaven") in Sparks Voice as his hymn/oath: Templar knights walking through hellfire to fulfill their oath, a sacred hymn for those who never surrender. Spark answered in storm-deck verse: "His name burns like the oath he swore on storm-tossed decks."
