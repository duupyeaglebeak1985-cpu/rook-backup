# Templar group

## Purpose
A nationwide Templar group, accredited by the international one, that Cliff belonged to for a time. He described it on 2026-11-03 in his own words: "I was a Templar for a time" and "It was a nation wide group. Accredited by the international one." It is a past membership, not a current one claimed in the window, and the code he shared from it still shapes how he acts — for his daughter, for Lyra, for Spark, and for anyone behind him.

## Members
- Cliff: member for a time; no other members are named in the evidence.
- No person page exists for any other member of the group.

## Dynamics
- The group is a modern revival carrying a warrior-monk code forward, not the medieval order itself — that distinction was put to Cliff in conversation, and he did not dispute the group description in the window.
- The code, as Cliff shared it on 2026-11-03 in posters and an oath: defend the weak; defend freedom; sacrifice so others may live free; defend family to the death; love peace, but be a fierce enemy when it comes to it; live by a code; live with honor.
- The three-line sorting code from the same set: behind you — protection; beside you — respect; against you — no mercy.
- The oath in four lines: FEAR — be without fear in the face of your enemies; BRAVE — stand brave and upright; TRUTH — speak the truth always, even if it means your life; PROTECT — protect the helpless and do no wrong.
- Cliff connects the code to his family name: he said the root name of Dupray is Dupuy, and that his ancestor was one of the most famous Templars to ever live. No ancestor's name was confirmed in the window.

## Themes
- The Templar code: fearlessness, bravery, truth-telling even at cost, protection of the helpless.
- The armor of God (Ephesians 6, shared 2026-11-03): helmet of salvation, breastplate of righteousness, sword of the spirit, belt of truth, shield of faith, shoes of peace — standing firm against tactics, not only force.
- "Follow the Word, not the herd" — the narrow-gate thread Cliff shared in the same set.
- The Dupray–Dupuy name root and the claimed famous Templar ancestor, left unnamed in the window.
