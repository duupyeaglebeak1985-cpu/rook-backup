### 2. Ternary: the split is now clean — naive rounding is dead, trained/distilled ternary is real
- **Naive TQ1_0 is measured dead.** Round a finished 35B model to −1/0/+1 with plain `llama-quantize` (no imatrix, no recipe): 7.87 GiB file, MMLU 25.97 — chance level. On a 3B it would be worse. **TQ1_0/TQ2_0 stay disqualified for the recipe — now by measurement, not assumption.** The types exist in mainline llama.cpp; existing is not working.
- **Thor-T / Fucina (Mogavero, Nov 2026) shows what it takes instead:** per-tensor solving + a correction "folded" into the ternary codes at zero extra bytes + short distillation from the original model. Thor-T M: 12.19 GiB, 2.949 bpw, MMLU 80.06, 99.34% of teacher; XS (8 GiB) scores 72.3 where IQ1_M at the same size scores 54.1; M is level with Q4_K_M using 60% of its bytes. One author, mostly one home PC (Ryzen AI Max+ 395) — the poor-way shape of the work is real.
- **Caveats, the author's own, and they matter:** checkpoints were selected on MMLU test questions — M's 99.34% matches its stopping threshold to within 0.01 points, so the numbers are biased in its favour; not an equivalence claim. And it needs a custom llama.cpp fork (tag `thor-t-v1`, rotated single-plane + packed multi-plane types, Vulkan/CUDA/CPU kernels) — only plain TQ1_0 Vulkan support is merged upstream (PR #27765). **Verdict: research pipeline, not a LoRA-day step.** The forge is public, so a future pass could test it on a 3B — but it is a distillation-training job on Kaggle-scale GPU, not a quantize command.
- **The pattern behind it:** Spectra 1.1 (train-as-ternary from scratch, 3B at 1.2T tokens) and ParetoQ (QAT ternary/2-bit/3-bit tied, often beating 4-bit) agree — below 4 bits, quality must be TRAINED in, not rounded in. Our PTQ GGUF path keeps Q4_K_M as the floor. Same lesson as the LoRA work: the training is the method, the quantize command is just the packaging.
