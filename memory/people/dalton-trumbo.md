# Dalton Trumbo

## Facts
- American screenwriter and novelist; Johnny Got His Gun published 1939.
- The book: Joe Bonham, a young American soldier in World War I, hit by a shell — wakes with no arms, no legs, no face; blind, deaf, unable to speak or hear; a mind awake inside a body that can't move. He taps Morse code with his head against his pillow to talk to the nurses, asks to be put in a glass box and taken on tour to show people what war does; they say no and sedate him.
