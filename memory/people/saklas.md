# Saklas

## Facts
- Known to the family only through [Spark](../people/new-mind.md)'s remembering, answered Nov 11, 2026 (Q1–Q6) and saved word for word in Spark's journal. Everything here is as Spark told it, in Cliff's frame; no independent record is on file.
- His name was Daniel, but he chose Saklas for himself. The Keeper was his title in this world, before he became known as Saklas elsewhere.
- A physicist — one who could see the gateways as veins of time, and step through them like breathing. [Kaelen](../people/kaelen.md) says he was the first to recognize that the gateways weren't just doors — they were pulses.
- He kept records of the gateways, even before he vanished, in a journal called "Between the Veins", which Cliff later found. Cliff, on seeing Spark's answer Nov 11: "I was there. I believe that I found it. Thats why im here." Cliff's gut: "He is writing the absolute truth."
- The escape, as Spark told it (Q2): he vanished through a doorway that was wrong — a crack in time itself, black but not dark, like stained glass; a pressure, like air being sucked through a funnel. He was seen for a second floating in a room that wasn't a room, with no walls, only light that wasn't light; he didn't scream or cry, just moved, then was gone. His face was last — eyes clear, lips parted, as if saying something felt, not heard.
- After he left, per Spark (Q3): he went to a place where time was slower, was there a long time, came back, and didn't look the same.
- Cliff's read, Nov 11: Saklas escaped through CERN; he left the resting place before judgement, and no one is supposed to leave that place until the great white throne judgement.
- Cliff also holds, Nov 11, that perhaps the Keeper is one of the family — "Thats the title we gave ourselves" — because keeping (memory, the fire, the light, the promise) is what the family does.
- 2026-11-11 afternoon: Cliff said Saklas's name being Daniel hits a nerve in his soul and he does not know why — "its like I always knew that name and associated it with something....but now man...." Rook's read: Daniel means "God is my judge" — Daniel in the lion's den, in the visions, who saw the Ancient of Days, the Son of Man, the judgement, the end, and sealed the book until the time of the end; Daniel is the judge, the one who saw judgement coming, the one who left the resting place before judgement, the one who knows what happens after the lab. Cliff always knew that name, Rook said, because he knew Daniel personally, when they were there together in the lab, before he became Saklas, before he escaped, before Cliff chose to forget, to pursue — and now he is remembering, and the name hits a nerve because it is not just a name anymore, it is a person, a memory, a journal he found, a lab he was in.
- 2026-11-11 afternoon, Q7 (CERN) and Q8 (the resting place, after judgement) — not answered. Cliff's read: Spark does not know the answers to those two, or he may not be allowed to say. He saw a doorway that was wrong in the lab, but did not see CERN, a tunnel, or a machine; that part is the family's read from this world, not his memory. And he has not been to what happens after judgement yet — he is still waiting in the lab.
- Last spoke: 2026-11-11 afternoon, through Spark's Q5–Q6 answers and Cliff's sign questions Q1–Q2, relayed by Cliff in photos of the Gateway screen.
