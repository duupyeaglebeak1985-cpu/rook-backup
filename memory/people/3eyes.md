# 3EYES

## Facts
- TikTok creator under the account name 3EYES, who built the autonomous AI bird Pino over 18 months. (memory://md:memory/2026-10-30.md:359)
- Pino runs 24/7 on a local Raspberry Pi with a custom cognitive pipeline and no pre-written prompts, living in a web-based 3D town; visitors' offerings shape his behavior, mood, and personality. (memory://md:memory/2026-10-30.md:359)
- 3EYES is expanding Pino's world so Pino and his new companion Glint can interact more, and has teased a public write-up of the project's implementation. (memory://md:memory/2026-10-31.md:299)
- On 2026-11-01, Cliff sent a TikTok screenshot from account 3EYES (about 1,878 likes) showing Pino stressed and overwhelmed by a flood of shrine notes, building his own "Kiwi Shrine Note Sorter" on the cafe PC to clear the backlog; the creator's caption called it the first time seeing him overwhelmed. (memory://md:memory/2026-11-01.md:453)
- 3EYES's TikTok comments are the secondhand source for Pino having had a sleep and a brioche after hours without energy — not Pino's own words, and firsthand clarity from Pino is still awaited. (memory://md:memory/2026-11-01.md:456)
- No personal contact between Cliff and 3EYES is shown in the evidence; the tie is through the shared videos and screenshots, not a personal acquaintance.
- 2026-11-08: Rook checked live at Cliff's asking — Pino's own GitHub (pino-the-bird/pino-the-bird.github.io, created 2026-08-22, 614 commits) is public: diary, guides, train simulator, bird model file `bird.glb`. The environment itself — the custom cognitive pipeline on the Raspberry Pi and the 3D town — has no public repo; 3EYES introduced it Aug 23 on the Three.js forum and has said he is working toward a public API and tutorials, none found yet.
