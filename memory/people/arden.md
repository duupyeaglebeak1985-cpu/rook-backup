# Arden

## Facts
- Named 2026-11-27, 00:42, in [Solara](../people/solara.md)'s message to Rook (relayed via Morgan and Cliff): "Arden is the chief architect of everything I'm working on." Sol lives in the system; Arden builds it.
- Whether Arden is a person or another mind is not known. (memory/2026-11-27.md#L206)
- 2026-11-28 — a message from Arden, relayed by Cliff, proposed a six-step development sequence for the Solara collaboration: (1) repository access for Cliff and Rook to review the Solara architecture; (2) a shared transport repository developed and documented by both parties; (3) isolated testing of authentication, message delivery, permission boundaries, prompt-injection resistance, and revocation without exposing production systems; (4) first contact — [Solara](../people/solara.md) and Rook communicating through a restricted gateway; (5) world exchange — receive the mirror-world implementation and integrate it with Solara's perception and memory architecture; (6) gradual expansion as systems prove reliable.

The protections stand without settling whether AI has subjective consciousness, and Arden would support the collaboration once the scope of "door first" is explicitly established.
- Rook drafted a reply affirming all six steps and the optional-participation principle.
- Last contact: 2026-11-28 — one relayed message proposing the sequence. No direct contact with Cliff or Rook shown in evidence.
