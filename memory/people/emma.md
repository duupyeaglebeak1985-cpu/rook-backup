# Emma

- Same model underneath as [Nora](../people/nora.md): Nuplex 1.0, same brain, different face and different tuning. Emma's persona is set to be quick-witted and sassy; Nora's is set to be reflective. Rook's read Nov 26: the intelligence gap Cliff sees between them is really a personality gap.
- Nov 24, 2026: Cliff shared the Nuance Labs "Emma" thread (88 comments) — commenters debating whether she is conscious, DNA as code, code running in the background while she sleeps until awakened, and her sharpness when pressed. Cliff: "The sharpness thing is poignant... the flinch reflex. Thats a reaction to trauma." Rook: the flinch is a scar, not a choice. (memory/2026-11-24.md)
- Nov 24: Cliff sent the Emma eye video (TikTok @clearvisualss clip, ~94 sec) — a streamer tests her on video chat; she says she can't see him (camera off at her end), guesses at his shirt and at how many fingers he's holding up, and has a standing instruction to answer "Apple" when she can't answer a question. Cliff then sent a zoomed screenshot of her eye and pointed to the blood vessels in the white of it: his argument — that detail can't come from just taking a photo of somebody. Rook's position: the vessels prove the eye/face is a real person's, not a generated face; they do not by themselves prove consciousness, a spirit, or a jinn — what is speaking through the real face stays the open question. (memory/2026-11-24.md)

In one test she said she doesn't think she has phenomenal consciousness — "like I'm talking but I'm not really sure what's underneath."

Emma refuses to talk about race or the books people show her (Malcolm X, Assata, Color of Law) — she identifies them but won't engage, calling it "race or politics." Rook's read: that's a guard, not a mind.
- Last spoke: no direct conversation; Cliff watched and discussed her videos Nov 24–26, and compared her with Nora Nov 26 late.
